before sending." The proof goes through `ServiceProfileController.view` →

  produced `422 INVALID_UNIT_ID` and a genuine red run; the fix was in the fixture, not production.

  `store.effectiveProfile(...).isPresent()` immediately before the second refusal.

  `check-pack.py` and `test_check_pack` red; confirm once with `git ls-files -v` and
