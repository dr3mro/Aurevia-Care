## Lessons for workers

- For a clause proof of already-built behavior, add only the test and doc; keep
  every test active and do not claim a `GAP:`.
- Confirm the `opencode.json` skip-worktree artifact once (`git ls-files -v`,
  hash against HEAD and `.github/opencode.ci.json`); restoring committed bytes
  temporarily in the worktree yields a genuine green tooling suite without
  editing or committing the protected file.
- Verify that a cross-tenant negative seeds an active permitted-role member in
  the other tenant, so a 404 is a real concealment rather than an authorization
  403 from a missing setup step.
