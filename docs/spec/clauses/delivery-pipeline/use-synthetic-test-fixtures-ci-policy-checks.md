## Clause

Use synthetic test fixtures, CI policy checks, dependency and secret scanning, signed builds, migration checks and monitored progressive rollouts.

## Status

This is one compound clause with six sub-items, and they do not share a status. Four have real, wired machinery that runs in this repository: synthetic test fixtures, CI policy checks, dependency and secret scanning, and migration checks. Two — signed builds and monitored progressive rollouts — need a cryptographic signer, a cloud deployment and human-observed evidence that the repository does not hold; under the acceptance rule those two parts are **Not built**, and their suppliers are named in [Gap](#gap). The clause-note convention for a mixed clause is `Partly built`: see [WEB-12 clause 2](../vulnerability-management/scan-dependencies-images-secrets-change-block-merges.md) lines 9-11, [OPS-05 clause 3](../monitoring/have-downtime-recovery-runbooks-paper-manual-capture.md) lines 9-11 and [WEB-11 clause 4](../release/use-expand-contract-migrations-feature-flags-default.md) lines 9-16, each of which splits a compound clause into a built half and a human-gated half.

The status is not `Built and verified`: the signed-artifact row at line 26 and the progressive-rollout row at line 27 of the [launch checklist](../../../operations/production-readiness/launch-checklist.md) are both `open`, line 33 is the still-open G5 sign-off row, and no SBOM, signature, deployment, rollout record or G5 acceptance is held in the repository. It is also not `Not built` overall: the four machinery sub-items below are implemented and executable, so calling the whole clause Not built would erase evidence that is genuinely present. The single green line `S-G5-07: OPS-06 evaluator unit tests and fixture checks passed` is a **false green** for the signed-build and rollout sub-items, read with the boundary in [Evidence](#evidence).
