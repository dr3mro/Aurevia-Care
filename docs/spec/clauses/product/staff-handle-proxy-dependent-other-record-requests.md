## Gap

Missing: an approved (signed) manual procedure owned by the site privacy and clinical authority; a proxy/dependent authority model with verification at the trust boundary; a staff intake and independent-approval workflow bound to a specific disclosure with a durable audit record (OPS-02); an HTTP entry point with a regenerated OpenAPI client; and a G5 site evidence artifact showing staff actually handled a representative request. The existing generic support case and the `POST /copy-requests` route have none of the authority verification, approval binding or policy-approval evidence the clause requires; the latter only records and routes a request.

Files that would change once the work is authorized: a new business-domain package under `apps/api/src/main/java/com/aurevia/` (named for what it holds, for example `patient/proxy` or `records/request`, never for a story or gate ID), a tenant-owned Flyway migration with RLS and FORCE RLS, a `Jdbc*` store behind a `*Store` interface, an injected controller with HTTP integration tests, the OpenAPI entry and regenerated client, a staff-web intake and review feature, and a site-owned procedure and evidence document under `docs/operations/`.

This needs at least four separate tasks: (1) a human-owned task for the approved manual procedure, market policy binding and G5 acceptance; (2) a backend slice for the authority model, durable store, controller, OpenAPI and 100% coverage tests; (3) a staff-web intake and independent-review slice; (4) an operations and evidence slice for the G5 record-request review artifact. The first task cannot be delivered by code and its evidence is not in this repository.
