# OPS-05 clause 4 — Prevent duplicates when recovering

## Clause

Prevent duplicates when recovering.

## Status

Partly built

## Evidence

- Requirement source: [PROJECT_V1_SPEC.md](../../../../.opencode/config/PROJECT_V1_SPEC.md) row OPS-05 names this sentence; the section 9 G5 row and section 10 decision "Service capacity and recovery targets" name the human evidence and owners.
- [Restore procedure](../../../../docs/operations/recovery/restore-procedure.md) section 5 prescribes the mechanisms: idempotency keys scoped to tenant, caller and command with a request digest; durable business uniqueness for external clinical actions after a retry key expires; deduplication of external events by immutable event ID inside the sender/type namespace with per-aggregate version; and reconciliation of supervised back-entry against entries committed before the outage. [Runbook](../../../../docs/operations/recovery/runbook.md) repeats the identifier-confirmation and event-deduplication rules.
- [Operational runbooks](../../../../docs/operations/recovery/operational-runbooks.md) require the paper/manual capture and supervised back-entry runbooks to reconcile re-entries against already committed entries so a chart note, order, result or release is not duplicated. ## Gap

- The human portion is not built in this repository, so the status is not "Built and verified" even though the mechanisms and the checker exist. No `kind: conducted_drill` artifact, observed supervised back-entry reconciliation against committed entries, duplicate-prevention incident record, named rota or site G5 acceptance is held here. Suppliers: the operational on-call owner and site clinical coverage owner run and record the drill in [drill-evidence.md](../../../../docs/operations/recovery/drill-evidence.md); the privacy and accountable operations owner records approval to reopen writes; engineering with operations supplies measured RPO and RTO; and the Product Owner, Clinical lead and G5 acceptance owners sign off under `S-G5-12` (`tasks/00258000-S-G5-12.md`). The open rows in the [launch checklist](../../../../docs/operations/production-readiness/launch-checklist.md) and [site launch approvals](../../../../docs/launch/site-launch-approvals.md) confirm no such evidence is recorded.
- No check proves prevention across the recovery boundary itself. The evaluator checks a declared boolean, not behavior: no test replays a command or an external event after restore and reopening of writes. If that becomes a machine-checkable obligation, the files would be [recovery_evidence.py](../../../../infra/recovery/recovery_evidence.py), [test_recovery_evidence.py](../../../../infra/recovery/tests/test_recovery_evidence.py) and [recovery-evidence.md](../../../../docs/operations/recovery/recovery-evidence.md); the current schema has no field for an observed post-restore replay outcome.
- A residual duplicate path sits outside this clause's built mechanisms: [use-durable-client-message-ids-deduplicate-retries.md](../messaging/use-durable-client-message-ids-deduplicate-retries.md) is "Not built" because conversation replies carry no durable client message ID, so a retry after recovery can duplicate a patient message.
- Files that would change to close the gap, all outside this documentation-only task: `docs/operations/recovery/drill-evidence.md` for the conducted drill record; a sanitized conducted-drill artifact produced in an authorized environment (not in this repository today); `docs/operations/production-readiness/launch-checklist.md` and `docs/launch/site-launch-approvals.md` for the open evidence and acceptance rows; optionally the evaluator, its tests and schema above if the observed replay is made machine-checkable; and, for the message gap, the conversation service and repository, a new migration, `contracts/openapi.yaml` and the regenerated client.
- Three separate tasks are needed: an authorized restore and downtime drill producing the sanitized artifact (the template already exists in `tasks/00103000-READY-54.md`), the human G5 acceptance under `tasks/00258000-S-G5-12.md`, and a cross-boundary code story for durable client message IDs (the gap is described in `tasks/00672000-SPEC-ARC-07-C06.md`). Optional evaluator strengthening can be folded into the drill task. None of these is in this task's scope.
