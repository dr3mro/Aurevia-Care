## Lessons for workers

- On a "did not finish cleanly" fix attempt, verify the committed work is actually broken before
  rewriting it: re-run the focused test and the relevant policy scripts, then inspect the
  integrator gate stages individually. Green work usually means the prior run's local gate was
  tripped by an environmental false positive, not by a defect.
- A cloud-worker `opencode.json is stale` failure is the CI-profile/skip-worktree artifact
  OPS-CW-003-01. Confirm once with `git ls-files -v opencode.json` and a throwaway-directory
  regeneration diffed against `git show HEAD:opencode.json`, then let the integrator run the full
  gate instead of editing the protected generated file.
- `check-pack.py` runs inside `scripts/quick-check.sh`; because the worker image masks
  `opencode.json` under skip-worktree, that stage fails for every worker in this image. Record the
  evidence and move on to the individual stages rather than treating it as a change defect.
- Confirm with `git show HEAD:<path>` before attributing `git grep` conflict markers to your own
  merge; do not edit out-of-scope files to "clean" them.
