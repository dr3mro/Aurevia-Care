## Summary
When the trusted integrator's protected-main push loses a race to a newer `main` with
non-tracker changes, publication now fails with an allow-listed, SHA-only recovery
signal instead of an unclassified traceback. A tokenless recovery job reuses the
existing conflict resolver, a trusted job pushes the resolved branches, and one
bounded re-dispatch re-validates only the signalled PRs on the new base.

## Findings
The original job failed with a non-fast-forward push after `main` advanced to 9ac6892d;
PR #1787 stayed open and no stale-gated code merged. The dead third-push retry branch
and loop were removed because every push failure is classified before a second attempt.

## Lessons for workers
A write-token publication job must never re-gate candidate code; emit a bounded,
allow-listed signal and let a fresh tokenless runner rebase, resolve and re-validate.
