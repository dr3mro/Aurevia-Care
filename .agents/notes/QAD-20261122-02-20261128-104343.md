## Summary

Closed the QAD/AUD/FIX finding that two `fileName` validators disagree. `DocumentInputs.fileName` now delegates to the single shared `UploadValidation.fileName` guard and its duplicate regex and `Pattern` import are deleted. The shared guard also rejects whitespace-only names, so a name can never escape the tenant object prefix through a blank/trimmed name. Dot-dot names (`..`, `a..b`) are rejected on `POST /api/v1/documents/associations` with `422 INVALID_FILE_NAME`, as they already were for artifacts, attachments and outage evidence. One bounded, path-free, control-character-free, no-parent-traversal rule now serves ARC-06, MSG-03 and X-03.

## Findings

- Decision (plan-reviewer and senior-backend, read-only reviewer evidence): full delegation, not a retained local 180/blank guard. A local guard would keep two rules that can drift again. The brief's risk statement that `UploadValidation.fileName` already rejects blank was inaccurate: a single space matched `{1,200}` and trimmed to empty. Adding `value.isBlank()` to the shared guard fixes that for every caller.
- Intentional behavior change: the document-link `fileName` bound widens 180→200 and the returned name is now trimmed. In-scope `DocumentsNegativePathsTest` was reconciled 181→201 and the new tests pin 180/181/200 accepted and 201 rejected.
- The remaining out-of-scope assertion `apps/api/src/test/java/com/aurevia/it/documents/DocumentInputsTest.java:76` still expects 181 characters to be rejected and will fail until the follow-up below merges. This is a documented integration dependency, not a skipped test: the worker's targeted suites deliberately exclude `DocumentInputsTest`, and the integrator must merge the follow-up before the full `mvn verify` gate can be green. Plan-reviewer accepted this sequencing with GO after the dependency was recorded.
- `check-wiring.py` found no violations and no resolved baseline entries; the deleted field was private and uncalled outside the deleted method.

## Lessons for workers
