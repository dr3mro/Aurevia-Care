## Clinical and architecture review
No patient-identifying data in fixtures; all SHAs, subjects and MRNs are synthetic.

## Findings
The short-TTL IdempotencyFilter owns frozen replay and 422 conflict inside its window;
the durable client id owns post-TTL and concurrent deduplication with 409 on a body
mismatch. The reply route is excluded from frozen replay so every retry re-checks live thread access; no replay-route registration remains.

## Lessons for workers
A keyed retry is only correct when every layer in the request chain agrees on one id
and one authorization decision; adding a header without registering the replay route
silently turns a retry into a 404.
