The consuming files (`messenger/MessengerPanel.tsx`, `mail/MailScreen.tsx`) are

component.

## Lessons for workers

- When a story names an absent scope folder and a requirement whose text does
  not match the outcome, record both immediately and ask for the wiring task;
  a perfectly covered exported component is not a delivered user-facing change.
- Check the shared breakpoint contract before implementing a "single-pane at
  768 px" request: 768 px is `medium` here, and `compact` is below it.
- Logical properties must be complete: `overflowX: hidden` on a grid root both
  breaks RTL-only styling claims and makes the root a scroll container that
  defeats sticky docking. Prefer wrapping plus fractional tracks.
