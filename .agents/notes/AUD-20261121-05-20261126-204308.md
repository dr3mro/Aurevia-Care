## Lessons for workers

- Before designing a constructor change, grep the **whole** test tree for direct construction of that service, not just the declared scope. A machine-enforced scope turns a two-line fixture update into a hard blocker, and no reflection seam or in-memory fallback in `src/main` is an acceptable substitute.
- When the owner question tool is cancelled in a non-interactive run, decide from the written rules and record the assumption and rejected alternatives with exact file paths and gate lines; do not silently widen scope or add a suppression.
