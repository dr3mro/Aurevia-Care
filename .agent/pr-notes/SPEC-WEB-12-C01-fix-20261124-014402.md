## Lessons for workers

- A Delivery failure on your PR head is not automatically your change's fault. Check the run's plan/duration and whether any job started before assuming a test regression; a 1-second run with all jobs waiting is a workflow validation error.
- Confirm ancestry with `git merge-base --is-ancestor` before assuming a known fix is missing from a failed head; here the fix was already present, which redirected the investigation.
- Protected-file blockers (`.github/workflows/*`, `scripts/*`, `AGENTS.md`, `.opencode/*`) must be documented and handed to the trusted owner, never edited in a swarm PR. Record the owning task ID instead of filing a duplicate task-request.
- When a reviewer reports an Optional stale line pointer, either fix it and re-review or leave the artifact byte-identical and record the exact correction; do not silently half-fix a reviewed document.
- `check-docs.py` and `check-secrets.py` print nothing on success — record "exit 0, no output" rather than inventing a final line.
