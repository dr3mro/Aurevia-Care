## Lessons for workers

- For an unpublished draft, `AttachmentService.download` checks `state().downloadable()` before
  `participant()`, so a wrong-unit actor gets 409 `NOT_PUBLISHED`, not 403 `WRONG_UNIT`; a wrong-tenant actor
  gets 404 `NOT_FOUND` because `load()` conceals the row first. Pass `uploaded.version()` or `requireVersion`
  fires before either guard and yields `STALE_VERSION`.
- Draft visibility compares `actor.subject()`, not `patientId`: a second actor needs a different subject, not
  just a different patient link, to get the 404.
- V82's FORCE RLS applies to the migration owner too: a raw-SQL proof must `SET app.current_org` in that
  session or the UPDATE matches zero rows and never reaches the immutability trigger.
- Compare read-back rows field by field, not with whole-record equality: `Clock.systemUTC()` nanoseconds and
  `timestamptz` microseconds make `createdAt` differ after a JDBC round trip.
