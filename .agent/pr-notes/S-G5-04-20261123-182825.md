## Lessons for workers

- Before adding a register to a launch pack, read the sibling packs. `clinical-approvals.md` and `release-and-recovery-rules.md` already define the exact status legend, the six-column register and the placeholder-token synthetic example; mirroring them avoids inventing a second format.
- `docs/launch/policy-pack.md` owns the shared OPS-01 topics and already assigns "supplier and partner obligations, contracts, credentials and outage drills" to S-G5-04. Cross-reference it rather than duplicating or contradicting its rows.
- When two packs use different `blocked` definitions, keep the legend the brief mandates verbatim and add a one-line cross-reference; do not silently switch semantics or mark a human prerequisite `blocked` under an engineering-gap legend.
- For a docs-only story, reproduce the gap and prove the fix with executed inline assertions (row/column/status counts, required-artifact names, no invented values) instead of a unit test, and state explicitly why 100% coverage is N/A.
- Verify every relative link exists before running `python3 -I scripts/check-docs.py`, and derive the valid requirement-ID set from the specification instead of guessing IDs; the checker fails on broken links and unknown IDs.
