# BUILD-65 — Cross-cutting scenarios and the first implementation slice, end to end

## Summary

Six new test-only acceptance classes plus one shared fixture compose the existing
production services with synthetic doubles. They prove one signed laboratory order
running through two clinics and a partner lab, and the five cross-cutting safety
scenarios from `PROJECT_V1_SPEC.md` §11. No production file, migration,
contract, script or tracker was changed. The earlier plan review raised Required
gaps (§11 line 200, the JaCoCo premise, notes completeness and reachability);
all were resolved and the reviewer answered `VERDICT: GO` before coding.

IAM-01 (active membership/role and tenant scope on every action), CLN-02
(reception verification, booking and check-in), LAB-01 (accession and specimen
match), XCH-06 (immutable revision, correction and approved release), PAT-02
(patient sees only a live, verified, effective revision). Spec §11 lines 193-199
and the first-slice paragraph at line 202.

## Authorization boundaries and audit events

Every scenario binds `TenantContext` (organization, subject, purpose, correlation)
and clears it in `@AfterEach`. Two or three synthetic organizations reuse identical
local facility code `MAIN`, MRN `MRN-SHARED` and subject spellings; the peer-tenant
assertions can only pass when the context is actually enforced. Asserted boundaries:
role denial (403 `FORBIDDEN`), wrong care unit (403 `WRONG_UNIT`), wrong tenant
(404 `NOT_FOUND` / empty list), stale revision (409 `STALE_REVISION`, 412
`STALE_VERSION`), duplicate/stale reauth (401 `STALE_REAUTH`), unstaffed critical
backup (409 `ROSTER_GAP`, 422 `BACKUP_ROSTER_GAP`) and duplicate publish (409
`IDEMPOTENCY_CONFLICT`). Audit facts asserted: staff invitation, patient
registration, check-in, critical escalation/acknowledgment, roster gap,
XCH04 review acknowledgement with one notification per original recipient,
patient report list/download/withdraw and delivery dead-letter review/replay.
No PHI, credential or real identifier appears in any fixture or note.

## Clinical and architecture review

Simulated/static only. The first-slice scenario touches a signed laboratory order,

## Findings

- The repository has no literal lab-order `sign(...)` method. The first-slice test
  uses `OrderPlacementService.place` (immutable, idempotent placement) as the
  signing substitute and `ResultRoutingService` import/verification as the
  reported attestation; this is recorded in the class Javadoc.
- There is no "suspend link" primitive. `PatientAccessService` exposes
  `sensitiveChange(UNLINK_CHART)` with single-use step-up reauth plus
  `checkChartAccess`, and `MessagingGovernanceService.recordRevocation` records an
  already-revoked connection. The dispute test uses the identity-dispute support
  case plus `UNLINK_CHART`; a privacy officer can review the case but `RESTRICT`
  is granted to `tenant_admin`, not `privacy_auditor`, so the suspension itself is
  modeled as the patient's step-up unlink after the officer's review. No duplicate
  primitive was added in scope.
- No dedicated "replay review queue" class exists. The failed-exchange test uses
  `DurableEventService.replay`, `reviewDeadLetter` and `deadLetters`, with
  `ResultRoutingService` proving the single report/review effect.
- Committed conflict markers remain in Javadoc comments of
  `apps/api/src/main/java/com/aurevia/exchange/hl7/{Hl7ParseException,Hl7Parser,Hl7Segment}.java`
  on `main` at HEAD (tool evidence: `git grep -nE '^(<<<<<<<|>>>>>>>) '`). They are
  inside comments and do not affect compilation; they are outside this task's scope
  and were not edited.
- Cross-referenced existing evidence, not duplicated: `CriticalResultEscalationAcceptanceTest`,
  `PatientRevisionAccessAcceptanceTest`, `PatientConversationAcceptanceTest`,
  `FinalReportReplayIntegrityAcceptanceTest`, `PatientEnrollmentAccessAcceptanceTest`
  and `PatientInstructionAcceptanceTest`. The genuinely new evidence is the
  end-to-end first slice across all stages and the tenant/negative branches listed
  in Coverage above.

## Lessons for workers

- Compose acceptance tests from the existing production services with mocked
  repositories and the shared `doAnswer` transaction callback; do not introduce a
  new store or helper in `src/main` for a scenario test.
- Read-back is part of the command: `ConversationService.open` and
  `LabAccessionService.collect` re-read their writes, so stub `find`/`findById`
  with enough consecutive answers for the whole sequence.
- `DurableEventService` If-Match is the event's `aggregateVersion` (starts at 1),
  not the attempt counter; `replay` and `deadLetters` require an admin role while
  publish/mark transitions accept a worker.
- `ReleasedReportService.download` fails closed on a future effective date; keep
  the synthetic effective date before the fixed clock.
