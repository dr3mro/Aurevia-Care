## Lessons for workers

- Before starting an audit (`AUD-`) story, `grep TODO.md` for its pattern or title: the daily
  audit can emit two tasks for one finding, one with a correct scope and one with a broken one.
  A broken scope plus a valid sibling task means stop and hand off, not improvise.
- When a story's "Expected fix" names a directory that its `scope` does not contain, treat the
  authoritative TODO.md scope as binding. The story text is a description, not permission to
  override AGENTS.md rule 3.
- A shared primitive must live in a package both callers may import; placing it in `patient` or
  `lab/accession` to satisfy a narrow scope would create the exact cross-domain coupling and
  competing primitive the story warns against.
